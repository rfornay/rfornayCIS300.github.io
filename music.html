Explore RaeHannah Music



Rae Hannah Music is a recent instagram page that I started to allow me to share my hobby and passion of music with others. I grew up singing and playing the piano, recently though, I picked up guitar as a result of a class elective.
I enjoy many genres such as pop-rock, alternative/indie, brit pop, folk pop and many more. I hope to grow this page and continue sharing covers of the songs I love, as well as dip my toe into songwriting one day!
